Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Rippl is a tool that takes one video, replaces copyrighted audio with a licensed track, reformats it for each destination, and publishes it to the social platforms you connect. This policy describes exactly what data that involves, where it goes, and what you can do about it.

Rippl is currently a limited beta operated directly by its founders, not a general-availability product. If you are using it, you were invited personally. The practices below describe the service as it actually works today, not as we intend it to work at scale.

Who we are

Rippl is operated by its founders. For any privacy question, correction or deletion request, contact umutboi43@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 7 days.

What we collect

DataWhy we hold it
Your email address and an optional display name To identify your account and contact you about the service.
Access credentials for platforms you connect — the OAuth refresh tokens and account identifiers issued by YouTube, TikTok and Instagram when you authorise Rippl To publish on your behalf after you have left the page. Without a stored credential, a scheduled post could not run.
Videos you upload, and any video Rippl downloads from a link you supply They are the input to the pipeline.
Audio derived from your video — extracted audio, the isolated vocal track, and the final mixed render Intermediate and final products of the audio processing.
Processing metadata — detected tempo and mood, chosen backing track, timestamps, status, error messages, and the post identifier and link each platform returns So you can see what happened to each post and we can diagnose failures.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics, tracking pixels or advertising identifiers.
  • No cookies beyond what is strictly required for a signed-in session.
  • No contact lists, follower data, direct messages, or the content of your social accounts beyond the posts Rippl itself creates.
  • No payment information — Rippl does not currently take payments.

How your video is processed

Audio separation and analysis run on our own servers using open-source models (Demucs for source separation, DeepFilterNet for noise reduction). Your video is not sent to a third-party AI service for this, and it is not used to train any model — ours or anyone else's.

Who your data is shared with

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising. It reaches third parties only where that is the point of the feature:

Each platform handles the data it receives under its own privacy policy. Your use of YouTube through Rippl is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Where it is stored, and how it is protected

Your data is held in a database and on disk on servers we control. Access credentials are stored so that scheduled publishing can run without you present; access to those servers is limited to the founders.

Being straight with you about beta-stage security. Rippl is an early build. Credentials are not yet encrypted at rest, and the service does not yet have a hardened multi-user authentication layer. Both are being addressed before Rippl is opened beyond invited users. Until then, please connect only accounts you are comfortable using in a beta, and revoke access (see below) if you stop using the service.

How long we keep it

  • Uploaded and rendered videos, and intermediate audio files are retained while your account is active so you can see your history.
  • Access credentials are kept until you disconnect the platform or delete your account.
  • Processing metadata is kept for your post history.

Ask us to delete any of it at any time and we will, within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Disconnect a platform. Revoking Rippl's access stops us publishing there. You can also revoke directly in your Google account, TikTok settings, or Meta Business settings — that works even if Rippl is unreachable.
  • Delete your data. Email us and we will remove your account, stored credentials, videos and history.
  • Get a copy. Ask and we will send you what we hold.
  • Correct it. Tell us what is wrong and we will fix it.

If you are in the UK, EU or another region with statutory data rights, these apply to you regardless of this policy, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Rippl is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used Rippl, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and notify invited users by email before the change takes effect.
